# Fabric Fusion – AI-Based T-Shirt Design Platform (Concept)

Fabric Fusion is a conceptual product and venture pitch project developed as part of an entrepreneurship coursework program.

The idea explores how Artificial Intelligence can support personalized T-shirt design generation based on user preferences, enabling faster, more unique, and customizable fashion concepts.

---

## Project Nature

This is an academic concept project.

- Based on research, surveys, and market analysis
- No real business operations or deployment
- Focused on product design, validation, and pitching

---

## Core Idea

Fabric Fusion was proposed to address common issues in custom T-shirt design:

- repetitive and outdated designs
- time-consuming customization
- limited personalized fashion options

The project explored how AI-assisted design generation could simplify the early design process and improve customization at the concept stage.

---

## Proposed Solution

The proposed workflow combines user preferences with AI-assisted creative generation.

- user-driven customization
- AI-assisted design concepts
- faster design workflow
- affordable personalized fashion concepts

---

## How It Works

1. The user provides design preferences.
2. AI tools generate design concepts.
3. Designs are refined manually.
4. The final design is prepared for presentation or printing in the concept workflow.

The implementation used AI tools such as Copilot for design generation support.

---

## Research & Validation

The idea was supported by survey and analysis work.

- 10 users interviewed
- 70% showed interest in the concept
- Key issues identified:
  - time consumption
  - lack of uniqueness
  - high customization cost

---

## Business Model Overview

Revenue streams considered:

- direct T-shirt sales
- customization charges
- bulk orders

Target audience:

- students
- young adults
- fashion enthusiasts

Channels considered:

- social media
- online store

---

## MVP Concept

A basic concept workflow was tested:

- input-based design generation
- AI-assisted design creation
- manual refinement of designs

This was used to validate the product idea at a coursework level.
